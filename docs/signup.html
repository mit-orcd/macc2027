The Contest Prizes Sign Up

Sign up your team

Registration is open to teams from across the MIT community. Signing up takes a few minutes through the form below; you can update your entry's details later as your project takes shape.

Open the team sign-up form

The sign-up form is hosted as a Google Form and opens in a new tab.

What you'll be asked

  • Team name and members - teams of any size are welcome, including teams of one. Prizes will be split evenly.
  • A contact email - so we can reach you with onboarding details, office hours, and event announcements.
  • Your elected area(s) - each entry elects one or two of the seven areas in which it wants to be judged (see the prizes page); plans can change as the contest goes on.
  • Your technology stance - open-weight models, open-source harnesses, commercial models and agents, bring-your-own scaffolds, robust research software engineering, or deterministic, verifiable modeling; plans can change as the contest goes on.
  • A short description of what you plan to build - a few sentences is plenty; plans can change as the contest goes on.

Before you sign up

  • Prize finalists must open-source their work.1
  • The contest runs across the Fall 2026 term and finishes during IAP in January 2027, with the showcase and awards in late January.
  • Questions? Contact cnh@mit.edu.

1 For legal reasons, participants who choose not to or are unable to open-source their work will not be eligible for prizes. ↩
